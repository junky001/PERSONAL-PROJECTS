Our School

Our school is a vibrant community of learners, teachers, and staff who are dedicated to providing a high-quality education. We offer a wide range of academic and extracurricular opportunities to help our students develop their talents and interests.

Our school is a center of academic excellence and co-curriculum activities.

About Us

Othoro Mixed Secondary School is a well-established institution that has been providing quality education for many years. Our school is committed to fostering a love of learning and helping each student reach their full potential.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe and nurturing environment where students can thrive academically and personally. We aim to equip our students with the knowledge, skills, and values they need to succeed in life.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a leading educational institution that empowers students to become responsible citizens and lifelong learners. We strive to create a community of learners who are passionate about making a positive impact in the world.

Our Values

At Othoro Mixed Secondary School, we value integrity, respect, and excellence. We believe in fostering a culture of inclusivity and diversity, where every student feels valued and supported.
